# ⚡ Revenue Leak Scanner AI

> **Find Hidden Revenue. Recover Lost Leads. Grow Faster.**

Revenue Leak Scanner AI is a premium AI-powered business intelligence demo designed to help businesses identify potential revenue opportunities hidden inside their existing lead flow.

## 🚀 Live Demo

👉 https://mk906036875-create.github.io/revenue-leak-scanner-ai/

## 💡 What It Does

Revenue Leak Scanner analyzes basic business metrics and estimates potential monthly revenue that may be recoverable through better lead response and follow-up.

### 🔍 Revenue Scan

Users enter:

- Monthly Leads
- Average Response Rate
- Current Conversion Rate
- Average Deal Value

The scanner then estimates:

- Missed Leads
- Current Deals
- Potential Deals
- Recoverable Revenue Opportunity

## 🤖 AI Recovery Plan

After completing the scan, users can generate a practical recovery plan focused on:

- ⚡ Faster lead response
- 🔁 Automated follow-ups
- 🎯 High-value lead prioritization
- 📅 Multi-step follow-up sequences
- 📊 CRM lead tracking
